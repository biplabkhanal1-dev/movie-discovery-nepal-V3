4. About the app

Movie Discovery Nepal is an independent project made for people who love finding stories from Nepal and around the world. It brings movie and series discovery, watchlists, watch-order guides and community opinions into one place. The goal is to make choosing what to watch simpler while helping fans explore beyond the titles they already know.

App Developer: Biplab Lamsal
This is an independent fan-made project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TMDB, Marvel, Disney, or streaming platforms. Catalogue information and artwork are provided by connected third-party services where available.

5. Data, privacy & about

Your API keys and local-mode reviews are stored in this browser's local storage. Do not use a shared/public device for private reviews or keys. Cloud mode sends review data to the Supabase project you configure. This is an independent fan-made discovery tool and is not affiliated with Marvel or TMDB.

TMDB attribution: This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Posters and metadata are provided by TMDB where connected.
